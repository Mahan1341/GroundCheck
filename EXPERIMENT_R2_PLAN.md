## Diagnostic design and acceptance

R1's 27/28 unseen-parent result used the familiar record-identifier format; only 2/75 broader relations were separated. Test whether that reflects format dependence. Use authored closed worlds with exact batch quantities, exact elapsed trial durations, and first-opening years. These are transparent fictional facts, not claims extracted from RAGTruth. Avoid ambiguous minimum retention periods. No arbitrary natural document substitution is relabeled.

The deterministic generator `research/relations.py` is the semantic specification. Each parent has two distinct named entities and two values. Every answer/value and evidence/value assignment appears under both labels. Only the numeric slot is annotated as error when the target relation disagrees. Paired documents contain the same two values; record order is crossed, not correlated with labels. Values are not A/2A. Names, source IDs and parent groups are disjoint across fit (24), selection (9), diagnostic (12). All derivatives stay with their parent. No RAGTruth partition changes.

Evidence template families: fit uses `record` and `parallel`; selection's unseen family is `catalogue`; diagnostic's unseen family is `reported`. Known-format conditions deliberately reuse fit families with new parents and new values; these are labeled separately from double-disjoint generalization. Known-value/new-wording variants reuse fit values intentionally, never fit parents. Answer paraphrase, evidence paraphrase, number words, minutes-to-seconds, target position, 8/32 correct padding sentences, claim position, and multiple facts in one sentence are distinct transformations. Baseline and transformed conditions retain aligned identity for invariance differences. Same answer/evidence marginal balance, token offsets, source separation and manifest hashes are acceptance gates. Template independence means evidence rendering family, not independent natural domains or independent paraphrase authors.

Generated claim/slot offsets are privileged diagnostic annotations. They never enter detector inference. Span models score every answer token and use unchanged global max for response risk; diagnostic claim max and numeric-slot max are computed afterwards. MiniCheck diagnostic claim risk comes from its ordinary predicted segment overlapping the known claim; no invented token localization. Custom token/character/exact-span scorers are separate from response metrics and are not official RAGTruth scorers.

Read-only models: B7 inner42, B8 inner42 answer-only, C0, C1, B6 cleaned MiniCheck. Fixed thresholds from R1 natural inner selection. Save all predictions and per-transformation results, paired relationship accuracy, directed delta, claim delta, invariance, FP, localization, AUROC/AP, runtime/memory. Parent-level 2000 paired resamples with seed42; same draws across methods. Synthetic groups are not independent natural sources. Inspect semantic examples before inference; no score-based example acceptance.

## Matched loss pilot (provisional until read-only diagnosis)

If the expected format gap is confirmed, compare R2-C0 CE and R2-C1 CE + pair ranking, both initialized from exact B7 inner42 weights. Same 2568 natural inner-fit records plus 768 synthetic rows (24 parents x 2 families x 2 evidence orders x 2 padding conditions x 4 combinations), same order/batches/targets and optimizer schedule. Natural and synthetic rows occur in both arms. No new encoder/retrieval/aggregation or unrelated negation tasks.

One fixed continuation epoch, seed42, AdamW lr1e-5/weight_decay.01, BF16 autocast, gradient checkpointing, max_length2048, positive CE weight3.370202392225359. Pair batches contain the identical answer under supported and contradictory evidence. Natural batches contain two ordinary records. Microbatch2, accumulation4 (effective8); shuffle complete batches with seed, same in both arms. All windows contribute CE with existing overlap weights. Synthetic pairs must fit one window with aligned complete answer offsets; fail rather than silently drop tokens.

Pair score is mean FP32 log odds (hallucination minus non-hallucination logits) over aligned numeric-slot answer tokens. This is the minimal changed factual claim component; averaging over all correct words would conflict with token targets. Loss = softplus(1 - (score_bad-score_good)). Margin1 is one unit of log odds; lambda candidates are exactly {0, .1}, no search. Both arms compute the pair graph, lambda0 disables its gradients. Claim annotations affect only training loss and post-hoc diagnostic reports. Inference remains unconstrained max-token. Test alignment, gradients, direction and inference allowlist.

Use fixed B7 inner threshold for both new arms as the primary decision point. No hidden new threshold selection. Score natural648 and synthetic selection parents first. Predeclared continuation gate: C1 improves unseen-family selection paired accuracy by >=.05 and mean directed response delta; natural macro-F1 drop versus matched C0 <=.01 and versus B7 <=.02; natural token F1 drop <=.02; supported paraphrase FPR increase <=.05. If any fails, stop repetitions for this loss variant, diagnose and still publish its diagnostic results. Otherwise run both arms at seeds43/44, identical B7 starting weights (optimization seeds, not independent pretraining). Do not select a favorite seed. Diagnostic held-out templates do not determine this gate.
